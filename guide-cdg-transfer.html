CDG to Disney
In 10 Minutes

Taking the TGV high-speed train from Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG) to Disneyland Paris (Marne-la-Vallée – Chessy) takes just 10 minutes. Here's how to get from touchdown to the park gates completely stress-free.

1

🎟️ Booking Your Train Tickets

When to book

  • Pre-booking (recommended for peak season): TGV seats do sell out during busy months. If you pre-book, build in a 45-minute buffer post-landing for deplaning, immigration, and a quick restroom break. Add extra time if you're checking bags.
  • Booking on arrival: you can buy tickets on arrival via the Trainline app or at the station machines or ticket window once you land.
⚠️
Important policy note: TGV tickets are non-refundable. Only pre-book if your travel insurance covers missed connections due to flight delays, or if you prefer the peace of mind of guaranteed seats during peak travel windows.
2

🏃 Terminal Arrival & Station Walk

  • Transit time: depending on your arrival terminal, the walk to the station (Gare) takes roughly 3 to 7 minutes after exiting immigration.
  • Follow the signage: look for clear overhead signs reading "Gare" (train station).
💡
Pro tip: use the free restrooms inside the terminal before entering the station. Restrooms inside the train station require a small fee.
3

🚉 Navigating Terminal 2 Station

  • Which board to read: look for the departure boards. Ignore the green boards, those display arrivals.
  • Ticket backup options: if your phone loses signal or battery, buy physical tickets at the yellow or blue automated machines, or the customer service office nearby.
  • Alternative transfers: if you prefer door to door transit, official taxi ranks and Uber pickups are located right outside the main terminal exits. Never accept rides from unofficial drivers inside the concourse.
4

📍 Finding Your Spot on the Platform

  1. Check the main departure board: match your train number to your assigned platform letter or number.
  2. Understand platform boarding letters: check your ticket for your carriage (Voiture) number, then look at the overhead digital display screens on the platform level. They show a map of the incoming train aligned with alphabetical letters marked on the station floor (Sector A, B, C, and so on).
  3. Stand in your designated zone: walk to the floor letter corresponding to your carriage before the train arrives.
⚠️
Crucial warning: the TGV only stops for a few minutes. Standing in the wrong section, especially on a shortened train set, can cause you to miss boarding entirely.
5

🚂 The Journey & Arrival

  • Transit duration: around 10 minutes.
  • Destination station: Marne-la-Vallée – Chessy, the very first stop on your train journey.
6

🏨 Exiting at Marne-la-Vallée – Chessy

Once you take the escalators up from the platform, choose your path based on your accommodation:

  • Staying at an official Disney hotel: take the hotel connection exit. Free Disney Hotel shuttles depart roughly every 15 minutes.
  • Heading straight to the parks or Disney Village: head out the main exit. This brings you straight to Disney's central security checkpoint, where you can walk straight into the parks or Disney Village.
  • Staying off-site in Val d'Europe: follow the signs to the RER A platform inside the station for a 2-minute connection down to Val d'Europe.
