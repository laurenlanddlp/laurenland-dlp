Restaurant Review, Halloween 2026

The Unlucky Nugget: Trick or Treat?

Last year's hit Halloween restaurant is back, and this time the Ravenswood wedding reception has also taken over the popcorn stand out front and Last Chance Cafe next door. I ate my way through the menu so you know what's worth your euros.

Prefer to watch? Here's the full review on YouTube.

👻

What is the Unlucky Nugget?

For most of the year this is the cheerful Lucky Nugget Saloon in Frontierland. At Halloween, the Phantom moves in.

It continues the Phantom Manor story: Melanie Ravenswood's wedding never happened, and you're a guest at the reception, with a few spooky surprises while you eat.

⏰

Know Before You Go

  • No reservations. It's walk up only, first come, first served.
  • Open 11:30 to 21:30.
  • Order at the counter, then your food is brought to your table.
  • The Disney Dining Plan is accepted.
⚠️
Budget two to three hours, including the queue. Service was very slow on opening day. We waited half an hour with no drinks, and the table next to us waited an hour for dessert. It was the same story last year.
📜

Menu and Prices

Adult menu €30

A main, a dessert and a cold drink (33cl) or mineral water (50cl).

  • Spectral Burger: beef, Brie, Brie sauce, pickled cabbage and cucumber, rocket and a black bun. Double patty for +€4.
  • Grim Delight: crispy chicken strips with black cheddar sauce and sweet chilli sauce.
  • Haunted Hog Roll: pulled pork, caramelised onions, black cheddar sauce, pico de gallo, pickled sweetcorn and smoky ketchup.

All mains come with crinkle cut fries or a side salad.

  • Desserts: Happily Never After Cake, Cold Soul Sundae or Scary Berry Bowl.
  • Singing Cream upgrade (+€2): black choux with black sesame ganache and passion fruit.

Vegan menu

Love at First Fright (falafels, beans, split peas, lentils, Venere black rice, silken tofu sauce and pico de gallo) with the Haunted Garden Compote (blueberry compote, purple sweet potato and coconut cream, puffed rice and caramelised pumpkin seeds).

Kids menu €16

For ages 3 to 11.

  • Mains: the Playful Spoon Burger (beef, Doritos, Emmental, smoky ketchup, purple bun) or Lil' Spoons' Supper (crispy chicken strips, black cheddar sauce, smoked ketchup), with crinkle cut fries or cherry tomatoes.
  • Desserts: the Cold Feet Sundae (blackcurrant ice cream, cocoa biscuit pieces, blueberry topping) or Time for a Treat (a cocoa éclair, a drinking yoghurt or an organic apple puree pouch).
  • Drinks: apple-strawberry Juicy Water, milk or Vittel.
💍

The Theming

This is where it shines. Look for portraits of Phantom Manor in its heyday, a creepy wedding guest book, and Melanie Ravenswood above the stage with a pile of gifts (read the messages).

There's also the skull covered wedding cake on the top table, a Happily Never After banner, and her phantom father at the piano. Even the napkins carry the Phantom Manor wallpaper.

Trick or Treat?

🍸

The Cocktails

Melancholic Mojito €12.50, Macabre Wedding €12

Treat

The Melancholic Mojito has Trois Rivières white rum, Perrier, cherry blossom espuma and red pepper strands, and it's light and refreshing. The Macabre Wedding is gin based, with hibiscus, strawberry and rose, and it's really nice.

Both are very sweet, so skip them if you're not a sweet tooth. If you'd rather go alcohol free, try the Ghost's Breath or the Groom of Gloom (both €9.50).

🍔

Spectral Burger

Adult menu main

Trick

It looks great, topped with a little Phantom Manor shortbread, but it's very dry and tastes like a regular bacon cheeseburger. Not worth it for the price.

🥖

Haunted Hog Roll

Adult menu main, new for 2026

Treat

There's too much toasted bread, but the pork is tender and smoky, and the pico de gallo and pickled sweetcorn really lift it. I'd get it again.

🎂

Happily Never After Cake

Adult menu dessert

Treat

A light, moreish sponge with strawberry and vanilla mousses and a cream cheese icing. It's a generous slice, and eating it with the wedding cake on the stage feels really immersive.

💀

Singing Cream

Dessert upgrade, +€2

Treat

A black choux bun filled with earthy black sesame ganache, with sharp passion fruit cutting through it, topped with a white chocolate Phantom head. It's beautifully balanced and worth the upgrade.

The Pause Gourmande

☕

A Cheaper Way In €12

Don't fancy spending €30? The Pause Gourmande sweet break gets you a dessert with a cold drink or a hot drink, from 15:00 to 17:30.

💡
Swap your drink for a cocktail for +€3.
😱

Screaming Coffee

Included in the Pause Gourmande

Trick

Lavazza espresso, ube syrup, whipped cream, crispy raspberry, biscuit and chocolate sauce. It looked nothing like the photo and arrived lukewarm, like a coffee you forgot about. Skip it.

🍨

Cold Soul Sundae

Dessert

Treat

Blackcurrant ice cream, cocoa biscuit pieces and blueberry topping, finished with Phantom chocolate coins. It's simple but refreshing.

🫐

Scary Berry Bowl

Dessert

Trick

Berries, pomegranate and grape with coulis and lime and mint foam. It tasted like Listerine, and one bite was enough.

🍺

Blueberry Beer

Sold separately, €8

I'm not a beer drinker, but it was surprisingly sweet. If you like beer and blueberry, go for it.

Final verdict

The main menu is mostly a treat, and only the burger lets it down. The Pause Gourmande is weaker, but what it lacks in execution it makes up for in theming. For a cheaper way in, pair the cake or the sundae with a regular coffee, or swap in a cocktail for €3.
