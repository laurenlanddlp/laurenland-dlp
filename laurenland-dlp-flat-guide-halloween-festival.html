Free Guide, 2026

Your Guide to Halloween Festival

September 26 to November 1, 2026, the Disney Villains join Mickey and Friends for the park's Halloween season. Everything below is included in regular park admission, no extra ticket needed.

Via Disneyland Paris on Instagram

🎃

The Atmosphere

Main Street, U.S.A. gets a full seasonal transformation, pumpkins, autumn decor, and Halloween touches worked into every corner of the park. It's the kind of detail worth just walking through slowly and taking in, before you get to any rides.

🎭

Mickey's Halloween Celebration

The daytime parade, autumn-themed floats down Parade Road with the Mischief Makers in full seasonal costume. Check the app on the day for showtimes, it isn't always at the same slot.

💡
If you're there on Halloween night (October 31): expect extended park hours and extra entertainment added on top of the regular schedule. Worth planning your trip around if you can.
📸

Character Encounters

  • Dr. Facilier holds court in Frontierland. One of the better seasonal meet-and-greets, worth the queue if you're a villains fan.
  • Jack Skellington and Sally are back for the season, a returning favourite rather than a new addition.
  • The Mischief Makers, a roaming group of characters including Donald, dressed for the occasion and scattered through the park rather than fixed to one meet-and-greet point.
🍔

The Unlucky Nugget Saloon

The Lucky Nugget Saloon in Frontierland gets renamed and re-themed for the season, with a dedicated limited-time Halloween menu. If you want the seasonal food experience rather than just the seasonal snacks, this is the one to book or queue for.

🍬

The M&M'S Scavenger Hunt

Pick up the official M&M'S map (check Guest Relations or the park app for where to grab one) and hunt down six hidden doors scattered across the park's lands. Find all six and you unlock a sweet surprise at Boardwalk Candy Palace.

💡
Good one to do early in the day if you've got kids with you, or as a between-rides activity that doesn't need a queue.
🛍️

Merch & Extra Bites

Beyond the Saloon, there's a wider spread of seasonal food and drink across the park, plus exclusive Halloween merchandise while it lasts. Nothing here needs pre-booking, just budget some time to browse.
