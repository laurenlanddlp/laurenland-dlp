Top 10 Must-Try Snacks

Don't waste your stomach real estate on basic, overpriced theme park food. From brand-new bites at Adventure Way to treats you can't get at any other Disney resort on Earth, ranked from great to life-changing.

Download the PDF
10

🍦 Pineapple Soft Serve ("Dole Whip")

Café de la Brousse, Adventureland

Everyone knows the famous Dole Whip in the US parks, but most guests don't realise Paris has its own twist. It isn't officially Dole-branded, but it's a smoother vanilla soft-serve base topped with sweet pineapple sauce or juice. Super refreshing on a warm afternoon after trekking through Adventureland.

9

🌯 Small Chicken Burrito (€7)

Casa de Coco, Adventureland

Packed with tender pulled chicken, fresh pico de gallo, guacamole, melted cheese, and cilantro rice, this stuffed burrito delivers incredible value and serious energy for a big park day. It's warm, satisfying, and easily one of the best budget-friendly quick bites in Disneyland Park.

💡
Tip: pair it with the signature churros for a complete Mexican-inspired quick-service meal on the go.
8

🍰 The Classic Carrot Cake

Cable Car Bake Shop, Main Street, U.S.A.

When you need to escape the weather on Main Street, this cosy Victorian bakery is elite. Grab a slice and snag a booth inside, or sit in the covered arcades to enjoy it.

💡
Bonus: the cream cheese frosting is made with 100% French Kiri cream cheese, a rich local flair you won't get back home.
7

🥂 Main Street Champagne & The "Mimosa Hack"

Outdoor carts, Main Street, U.S.A. / Gibson Girl Ice Cream Parlor

Disneyland Paris is the only Disney park in the world where you can buy a glass of real champagne from an outdoor cart and stroll right up to Sleeping Beauty Castle with it. It runs around €20, which includes a keepsake plastic souvenir flute.

🍾
Lauren's ultimate hack: not a fan of straight champagne? Grab a small bottle of orange juice from Market House Deli or Gibson Girl Ice Cream Parlor first. Take a few sips of your champagne to make room, pour in the OJ, and there it is: the official Main Street mimosa.
6

🇫🇷 Authentic French Macarons

Fantasyland chalets & snack stalls

You're in France, don't skip authentic patisserie for a plain pre-packaged cookie. These macarons are delicate, beautifully themed, and delicious. At around €2 each, it's one of the cheapest and most cheerful sweet treats in the park.

5

🥞 Fresh Hot Nutella Crêpes

Les Chalets Gourmands

Kicking off the top five: fresh Nutella crêpes made right in front of you on the hot griddles, served piping hot, folded, and smothered in rich hazelnut spread.

🔥
Hot take: skip the Mickey waffles. They're served cold at Victoria's and pretty underwhelming compared to the fluffy waffles at the US parks. The crêpes give you the real French street-food experience, right inside the bubble.
4

🐟 Fish Fritters with Curried Tartare Sauce

Nordic Crowns Tavern, World of Frozen

Hear me out: four golden, ultra-crispy fish bites paired with a punchy curried sauce. Think high-end, protein-packed fish nuggets, but that curried tartare gives you a bold, savoury kick you don't expect in a theme park.

3

🥪 The Ultimate Croque-Monsieur

Market House Deli, Main Street, U.S.A.

The ultimate French comfort food: layers of melted ham, thick cheese, and rich béchamel sauce between toasted bread, topped with even more melted cheese. Warm, gooey, filling, and easily large enough to split for a quick lunch.

2

🥗 Fresh Burrata & Tomato Salad

Adventure Way

Ordering a salad at a theme park sounds crazy, but theme park food fatigue is very real. After a full day of sugar and deep-fried eats, cutting into a cold, creamy ball of burrata topped with ripe tomatoes, pesto, and balsamic glaze is pure bliss. It feels like a cosy Mediterranean bistro right in the middle of your park day.

1

🏆 The Freshly Filled Custard & Strawberry Donut

Adventure Way

The undisputed number one snack in Disneyland Paris. Forget dry, pre-packaged pastries sitting behind a glass case for hours, these are piped to order right in front of you with silky pastry cream and topped with fresh strawberries. The contrast between the soft dough, cold custard, and sweet fruit makes this the single best bite across both parks.
